Pop Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Pop Up ("Pop Up," "we," "us") runs the Pop Up mobile app. Pop Up helps people find local pop-up markets and events, helps sellers find events worth selling at, and helps hosts run their events. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. Questions? Email support@popupanalytics.app.

The short version

  • You can browse Pop Up without an account.
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account details, what you choose to add to your profile or events, your location when you use location features, and, if you sell, the sales data from the point of sale you connect.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not show ads, and the app has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

1. What we collect

Your account. Your name, email address, an optional phone number, and a password. We only store your password as a secure hash, so we never see it.

Your business profile (sellers and hosts). What you add to your profile: your business name, description, business email and phone, business location, website, social handles, photos, and your booth setups. When you agree to the Vendor Agreement, we keep the name you typed as your signature and when you signed.

Optional owner details (sellers). When you set up a seller profile, you can choose to add details about the owner and the business, such as the owner's name and age range, the business's nationality, ownership (for example woman-owned, minority-owned, veteran-owned, LGBTQ+-owned or disability-owned), and military status. All of these are optional. You decide which ones hosts can see, using the visibility setting next to each one. Hosts use them to find sellers for events that welcome them. We never sell them.

Location. If you allow it, the app uses your location while you are using it: to show events near you, to find events for you in Discover, and to confirm you are at the event when you check in to your booth. When you check in, we store the location and time of that check-in with your booth. We do not track your location in the background. You can say no to location and still browse; the map starts in a default area.

Photos. Photos you choose to upload, such as event, product, booth and setup photos, profile pictures, and permit or license documents. You pick them from your library or take them with your camera. We only receive the photos you pick.

Messages and posts. Messages between hosts and the sellers at their events, event announcements, and reviews and ratings you post.

Point of sale data (sellers). If you connect a point of sale (Square now, Shopify later), Pop Up reads, through that connection, your items, prices, item photos, and your sales (what sold, when, and for how much). We store a secure access key for the connection. We do not ask your point of sale for your customers' names, emails or phone numbers. Pop Up never moves money or changes anything in your point of sale.

Booth fee payments. Card and Apple Pay details are entered directly into Stripe's secure payment form. They never reach our servers. We keep a record of each payment (the amount, its status, and Stripe's reference numbers). Hosts who collect booth fees verify their identity and bank details with Stripe directly, not with us.

Saved items and activity. Events and sellers you save, the events you apply to or host, and your applications and their status.

Support, feedback and reports. When you send feedback, report something, or report a crash from the app, we receive your message, any screenshot you attach, your email (or "guest"), and your device model and app version.

What we do not collect. Your contacts, microphone, health data, or background location. Recent searches stay on your device; we do not keep a search history on our servers.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: show events and sellers, handle applications, check-ins, messages and booth fee payments, and show you your own numbers.
  • To show sellers their sales, and to show each event's host how the sellers at that event did.
  • To answer support requests and reports, and to keep the service safe and working.

Market statistics. Pop Up may also count sellers' sales in market-wide statistics, such as typical prices or what sells at which kinds of events. Before it is counted, the seller's name and business are taken off. We only show a statistic when enough sellers are counted in it that no one business can be picked out, and we never sell any seller's individual numbers. This is part of the Vendor Agreement a seller accepts when connecting a point of sale (see section 6 of our Terms of Service). If you do not want your sales read, do not connect a point of sale.

3. Who can see what

  • Everyone: published events, and a seller's public profile and tagged products.
  • Hosts: the profile, products and application of sellers who apply to or are invited to their events, the optional owner details each seller chose to show, and the sales of the sellers at their own events.
  • Not public: your email, phone, password, payment records, permits, and your sales outside the events you sell at.

4. Service providers

We use these companies to run Pop Up. They only use your data to provide their service to us.

  • Render: hosts our servers.
  • Supabase: our database and photo storage. Permits and documents are kept in private storage.
  • Upstash: short-lived background job data.
  • Stripe: booth fee payments, refunds, and host payouts.
  • Square and Shopify: the point of sale connection a seller chooses to make.
  • Slack: delivers feedback, reports and error alerts to our team.
  • Apple: maps, directions and address lookups in the app.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers. We may share information if the law requires it.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account. When you do:

  • Your personal information (name, email, phone and your optional owner details) is removed or anonymized, and your login stops working.
  • Business records such as past events, booths and sales history may be kept as anonymized records that are no longer linked to you, so hosts and other sellers' event history stays correct.
  • Records we have to keep for legal or accounting reasons, such as signed agreements and payment records, are kept only as long as needed for that.

Disconnecting your point of sale stops Pop Up reading new data from it. To ask for deletion another way, or with any privacy question, email support@popupanalytics.app.

6. Children

Pop Up is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your choices

  • Location, photos and camera: allow or turn off anytime in your iPhone Settings.
  • Owner details: add, hide from hosts, or remove anytime in your seller profile.
  • Point of sale: connect or disconnect anytime.
  • Account: create or delete anytime in the app.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
